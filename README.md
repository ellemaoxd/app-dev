# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

> "Friends don't lie." — Eleven

**Stranger Things** is an American science fiction horror drama set in the 1980s.
It follows a group of friends in the small town of *Hawkins* as they uncover secret government experiments and supernatural mysteries.

## Why I Recommend It
- Perfect blend of suspense, heart, and 80s nostalgia
- Deep, relatable friendships at the core of the story
- Each season builds on the last with high stakes
- Incredible soundtrack and retro aesthetic

### Main Characters
1. Eleven — telekinetic with powerful abilities
2. Mike — loyal and protective friend
3. Dustin — clever, witty problem solver
4. Lucas — practical, brave, and honest
